Privacy Policy

The Lab · last updated 3 September 2026

The Lab collects nothing. There is no server behind it, no account to create, and no analytics of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app stores

The Lab keeps what you log in your device's own browser storage: the compounds you track, doses and dates, bloodwork values, workouts, food entries, body measurements, and any cycle or mood entries you choose to record.

All of it is written to local storage on the device and read back from there. None of it is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, email address or phone number
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No network requests carrying your data, of any kind

When the app touches the network

Two cases, both of them things you start.

  • Links you tap. Every guide cites its sources, and tapping one opens that page at PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, DailyMed, the FDA or a journal. Those sites are then subject to their own privacy policies. The Lab sends them nothing about you beyond the ordinary act of opening a link.
  • Updating the app. The app checks for a newer version of itself when you open it with a connection, which is an ordinary request for the page and carries no data about you.

Notifications

Dose reminders and restock warnings are scheduled and raised on the device itself. Nothing is pushed from a server, because there is no server, and no notification content leaves the phone.

Your data is yours, and it is fragile

Because everything lives in local storage, clearing the app's data, uninstalling, or clearing browser data removes it permanently. There is no copy anywhere else and no way for anyone to recover it, including us.

Export a backup from Settings. It writes a JSON file you keep. Nothing is uploaded when you do.

Children

The Lab is not intended for anyone under 18 and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Medical disclaimer

The Lab is a logging tool. It summarises published research and records what you enter. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a recommendation to start, stop or change anything you take. Decisions about medication belong with a qualified clinician who knows your history.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new use of data.
